Est. lookups = number of individual text checks needed to close the row; 0 where the row is a labelling convention rather than a gap.

Status: Open = a stated gap still in the text and web-checkable; Open – print only = needs a physical TRB or RSV2CE; Open – n of m verified = a position-category row partly checked cell by cell; Closed = checked against the translation's own text (or, for position categories, every cell recorded as verified). Proxy checks (RSV Catholic Edition standing in for RSV2CE, the NRSV parent standing in for NRSV-CE) and honest-label rows no longer count as closed: from 2 Oct 2026 every row is either closed against the exact source named or open.

The ten translations checked at every Category C variant are AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT and RSV2CE; the 17 not checked there are AKJV, ASV, BSB, CJB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, RSV, Smith's Literal Translation, TRB, WEB, Webster's Bible and YLT.

Categories F and G (added 2 Oct 2026) track the doctrinal, ethical and lifestyle position categories across all 27 denominations and all 27 study Bibles -- 18 categories at first, 31 since 6 Oct 2026, when the 'Other divisive positions' table (13 categories) was added to both sections (ledger rows 97-109 for the denominations, 110-122 for the study Bibles). Since the same date the amber 'not independently documented' flag also catches the study-Bible label 'General pattern of the editorial tradition, not independently documented in the notes', which the earlier pattern missed.

Category H (added 7 Oct 2026) covers the Major Dividing Issues Among Believers section, one row per chapter (IDs 124-154, replacing the single row 123). Each chapter has four checkable parts, shown on the 'Divisive Issues x categories' sheet: the attributions in 'The main positions' (manual; lookups = bullets), the catalog cross-references in 'Passages each side rests on' (checked automatically by every build: every entry ID and variant § must resolve), the dates in 'Dated splits' (manual; lookups = distinct years), and the where-they-fall tables (generated at build, nothing to check). Manual checks are recorded in position_verifications.csv with Section 130, Entity = the column name and Category = the chapter name; the build refills the H rows from that file and the live text. Their 'What the text currently says' and 'Est. lookups' are refilled by every build from the live tables, less any cell already recorded as verified in build/verifications/position_verifications.csv (one line per entity x category: Section, Entity, Category, Status, Checked against, Date, Notes). The two matrix sheets show every cell's current source label with the verification overlay.

Since 7 Oct 2026 every build also produces a_Bible_study_primer_manual_checklist_vYYYYMMDDx.xlsx (build/generate_manual_checklist.py): the study-Bible cells not yet recorded as verified, for the volumes whose notes are not available online, with the verses to open, plus the open RSV2CE print rows. It is the hand-in-hand companion to this workbook and shrinks as position_verifications.csv grows.

Cells verified / Cells total (added 8 Oct 2026): for the F and G rows, the number of that category's checkable cells (those stating a position, with or without an independent source) recorded as verified in position_verifications.csv, out of the number of such cells across the 27 entries; for the H rows, the chapter's manually checked components (main positions, dated splits) verified out of two. Blank for categories A-E, whose unit of work is the row itself. The Summary sums these per category so progress is visible even while a row stays Open, since an F or G row closes only when every one of its cells is verified.
