Category H (added 7 Oct 2026) covers the Major Dividing Issues Among Believers section, one row per chapter (IDs 124-154, replacing the single row 123). Their 'What the text currently says' and 'Est. lookups' are refilled by every build from the live tables, less any cell already recorded as verified in build/verifications/position_verifications.csv (one line per entity x category: Section, Entity, Category, Status, Checked against, Date, Notes). The two matrix sheets show every cell's current source label with the verification overlay.

Since 7 Oct 2026 every build also produces a_Bible_study_primer_manual_checklist_vYYYYMMDDx.xlsx (build/generate_manual_checklist.py): the study-Bible cells not yet recorded as verified, for the volumes whose notes are not available online, with the verses to open, plus the open RSV2CE print rows. It is the hand-in-hand companion to this workbook and shrinks as position_verifications.csv grows.

Cells verified / Cells total (added 8 Oct 2026): for the F and G rows, the number of that category's checkable cells (those stating a position, with or without an independent source) recorded as verified in position_verifications.csv, out of the number of such cells across the 27 entries; for the H rows, the chapter's manually checked components (main positions, dated splits) verified out of two. Blank for categories A-E, whose unit of work is the row itself. The Summary sums these per category so progress is visible even while a row stays Open, since an F or G row closes only when every one of its cells is verified.

Labelling pass of 9 Oct 2026 (Section 110): every Source cell not yet verified that rested on a general pattern, local custom or an unnamed "official teaching" now says so in a uniform way -- the label ends "not independently documented", and labels that had read "Official teaching" or "Official position" with no document behind them now read "..., no document identified; not independently documented" (152 cells). **Catholic Study Bible (Oxford, 2nd ed., NABRE 2011).** Read 22 October as a scanned PDF supplied by the author (an OCR text layer over page images; running heads and note keys survive well enough to find any note by book, chapter and verse, and doubtful readings were checked against the page image). The volume has two layers: Oxford's Reading Guides, signed by their authors, which frame the Bible in Catholic terms, and the NABRE's own notes, which are the bishops' conference's historical-critical notes. Where a cell rests on one or the other the ledger says which. **Cultural Backgrounds Study Bible (Zondervan; NKJV edition 2017).** Read 22 October as an EPUB supplied by the author. The author supplied the NKJV edition rather than the NIV edition the entry names; the copyright page and editors' introduction confirm the notes are the same Walton–Keener apparatus, so the entry keeps its heading and records both editions. The EPUB is a clean ebook: one 'Annotations for <book>' file per book (a few books split across two files), articles set inline under their passage heading, book and genre introductions. The notes are background, not doctrine, so most cells quote what the volume says the ancients thought and where it places the biblical writer; a cell is marked 'not addressed' only after the relevant passages and a whole-text search were checked (inerrancy, cessation, just war, purgatory, Calvinism/Arminianism, prosperity gospel, gambling, contraception). Twenty cells moved from 'Not addressed' to a recorded position or description; see the changelog.

**NIV Study Bible (Zondervan, Fully Revised Edition 2020).** Read 22 October as an EPUB supplied by the author — the same HarperCollins ebook structure as the Cultural Backgrounds volume (one 'Study Notes' file per book or part-book, keyed by verse; some keys carry a leading symbol for archaeology, application or character notes; articles set inline under their passage with a 'Go to the Articles Index' tail). The volume's own introduction states the rule the entry now reflects: traditional evangelical theology, with 'an evenhanded approach by indicating those differences' where editors knew of them; so the contested cells quote the article that lays out the views, and the uncontested cells quote the note that rules. Whole-text searches run for inerrancy, gambling, dancing, abortion, contraception, cessation, pacifism, just war, purgatory, perpetual virginity, transubstantiation, Calvin/Arminius and prosperity gospel. All twenty-four 'general pattern' labels are gone; see the changelog for the nine cells corrected in substance.

**The Orthodox Study Bible (Thomas Nelson / St. Athanasius Academy, 2008).** Read 22 October as an EPUB supplied by the author. One file per book with the study notes held in eleven shared 'Endnotes' files and reached by anchor from the text, so the notes were mapped back to their books through the anchors; study articles sit inline under letter-spaced headings; Psalms follow LXX numbering (Ps 138 = 139). Notes are keyed by verse or range, and many Old Testament passages carry no note (Genesis 9:6, Genesis 38:8-10, Exodus 21:22 among them), which is why several ethical cells read 'not addressed' where the old entry had supplied Orthodox teaching from outside the volume. Whole-text searches run for abortion, homosexual, capital punishment/execute, gambling, dancing, contraception, inerrant/infallible, evolution, pacifism/just war, universalism, filioque, sola scriptura and icon.

**New Oxford Annotated Bible with the Apocrypha (NRSV, Fully Revised Fifth Edition, 2018).** Read 22 October as an EPUB supplied by the author. The author's instruction named the RSV Expanded Edition (1977); the file supplied is the NRSV fifth edition (Coogan, Brettler, Newsom, Perkins), so the entry is written for the edition in hand and its header records the line of editions. One file per book with the annotations inline, keyed by passage range ('3.21–26:') with verse glosses inside the block; the lookup therefore matched a verse to the range that covers it. The annotations are deliberately non-doctrinal — the preface calls them 'notes rather than paraphrase or commentary' — so most cells quote what the notes say about the text and add that no doctrine is adjudicated; 'not addressed' is used only after the passages and a whole-text search (abortion, gambling, dancing, contraception, inerrant/infallible, evolution, pacifism/just war, universalism/annihilation, purgatory, perpetual virginity, transubstantiation, Trinity, tithe) were checked.
