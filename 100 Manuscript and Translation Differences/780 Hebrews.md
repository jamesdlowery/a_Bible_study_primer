# Hebrews: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

A systematic search did not find a case in Hebrews where the ten tracked translations render a verse with different, meaning-changing wording. The most debated variant in the book, discussed below, is one where all ten translations agree with each other.

---

## Side note: a case where all ten translations agree, but the variant has attracted significant scholarly debate

- **Hebrews 2:9** — describing Christ's death, the well-attested reading is that he tasted death for everyone "by the grace of God" (*chariti theou*). A small number of manuscripts and patristic citations instead read "apart from God" (*chōris theou*) — a one-word difference that some scholars connect to early reflection on Jesus' cry of dereliction from the cross ("My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?"). The "apart from God" reading is generally judged poorly attested and secondary by modern textual critics. All ten tracked translations render "by the grace of God"; this research pass did not find evidence that any adopts "apart from God," even as a footnote.
