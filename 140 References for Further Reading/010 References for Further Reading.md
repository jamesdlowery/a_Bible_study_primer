# References for Further Reading

## About This List

This is a general bibliography of standard, widely respected reference works in the fields this book draws on — Old and New Testament textual criticism, the history of English Bible translation, and the popular-apologetics literature on alleged biblical contradictions. It is **not** a claim that any specific fact, figure, or wording comparison in this book traces to a specific page in a specific work below; that level of per-claim sourcing was not tracked during the writing of this book, and attempting to reconstruct it after the fact would risk misattributing content to sources that didn't actually inform it. What follows instead is an honest starting point for a reader who wants to go deeper on any of this book's major topics, organized by the section of this book each group of works is most relevant to.

Where a work is available in multiple editions, the most recent edition at the time of writing is listed; earlier editions remain valuable and are often what's available secondhand or in libraries.

## How the Entries Were Sourced

**This book is a finding aid, not a citable scholarly apparatus.** Its two catalogs tell you *where* the manuscripts and translations differ and *what* is usually said about a disputed passage, so that you can go to the primary sources and the standard reference works with a specific question. It does not carry a footnote per claim, and it should not be cited as if it did.

How the entries were built:

- **Translation wording** was checked against each translation's own published text (print or the publisher's official online edition) wherever that text could be consulted; entries say "not verified" where it could not be, chiefly where a translation's own text was unavailable for a given verse. The edition conventions are stated in How to Use This Book.
- **Manuscript evidence** (which witnesses read which way) is drawn from the standard critical editions and handbooks listed under Biblical Source Manuscripts below -- chiefly the *Biblia Hebraica* and Nestle-Aland/UBS apparatus as summarized in Tov, Würthwein and Fischer, Metzger and Ehrman, Metzger's *Textual Commentary*, and the Aland and Aland introduction.
- **Harmonizations** in Reportedly Contradicting Passages are drawn from the popular-apologetics and reference literature listed below (Archer; Kaiser, Davids, Bruce, and Brauch; Geisler and Howe; the NET Bible's translators' notes), with the critical-scholarship counterweight noted in that section's opening pages.
- **Denomination and study-Bible positions** are taken from each body's or edition's own published statements, catechisms, confessions, and editorial prefaces, as cited within the entries; adherent figures are from the 2020 U.S. Religion Census unless an entry says otherwise. From October 2026 the position cells have been checked one by one against those documents, and the documents actually read are listed under Primary Sources Consulted for the Position Tables below; the repository's verification ledger (`build/verifications/position_verifications.csv`) records, for every checked cell, the document and passage it was checked against.
- **Drafting** was done with AI assistance and single-editor review, as described in A Note on Method and Verification. Errors should be reported through the repository named on the title page.

## Sources for the Tracked Translations

**Sources for the 27 tracked translations.** Wording was checked against each translation's own text as follows. *Publisher's official online edition:* the New American Bible Revised Edition (bible.usccb.org, the U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops); the English Standard Version (esv.org, Crossway); the New International Version (biblica.com); the Legacy Standard Bible (read.lsbible.org). *Print edition:* The Readable Bible (TRB; Iron Stream Media, 2024), which has no searchable online text; every passage in the catalogs that cites it was checked against the print edition. *Public-domain texts consulted through aggregator sites (Bible Hub, Bible Gateway, Blue Letter Bible, StudyLight, Bible Study Tools, YouVersion / bible.com):* the King James Version, American Standard Version, World English Bible, Young's Literal Translation, Darby Bible Translation, Webster's Bible, American King James Version, Smith's Literal Translation, Douay-Rheims Bible, Catholic Public Domain Version, and Berean Standard Bible; and the NET Bible, which is freely available at netbible.org and on the same sites. *Copyrighted modern translations consulted through their publishers' licensed presentations on those same aggregator sites, or in print:* the Amplified Bible, Christian Standard Bible, New American Standard Bible, New Living Translation, Revised Standard Version, RSV Second Catholic Edition, New Revised Standard Version Catholic Edition, Easy-to-Read Version, and Complete Jewish Bible. *Inferred from a parent translation:* the New King James Version, whose readings, where cited, are inferred from its close textual relationship to the KJV rather than checked independently, and the AKJV wherever an entry says so. Where a translation's text was not checked for a given passage, the entry marks it "not verified" (or states an expectation as an expectation) rather than assigning it to a camp.
