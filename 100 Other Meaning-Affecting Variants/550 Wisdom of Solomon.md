# Wisdom (of Solomon): RSV2CE Compared Against Other English Translations of the Apocrypha

**Comparison translations:** RSV2CE, NABRE, Douay-Rheims, NRSV.

This research pass did not confirm a specific verse where RSV2CE and NABRE or Douay-Rheims render clearly different wording with a clearly different meaning.

---

## Background: why Wisdom is a poor candidate for this kind of split in the first place

Unlike Tobit, Judith, Sirach, and Baruch, the Wisdom of Solomon was composed directly in Greek (most likely by a Hellenistic Jewish author in Alexandria), not translated from a Hebrew or Aramaic original. It survives in a single, comparatively stable Greek manuscript tradition (the major Septuagint codices), without the competing recensions or Vulgate-vs-Greek divergence that produce real translation splits in Tobit and Judith. All English Catholic translations, including RSV2CE and NABRE, work from essentially the same underlying Greek text, and Douay-Rheims' Vulgate-based Latin is itself a fairly direct, non-abridged translation of that same Greek here (unlike its treatment of Judith). I would not expect a Tobit- or Judith-scale divergence to turn up in Wisdom, though I haven't ruled out smaller wording differences at the individual-verse level.
