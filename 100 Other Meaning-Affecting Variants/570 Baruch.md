# Baruch (including the Letter of Jeremiah): RSV2CE Compared Against Other English Translations of the Apocrypha

**Comparison translations:** RSV2CE, NABRE, Douay-Rheims, NRSV.

This research pass did not confirm a specific verse where RSV2CE and NABRE or Douay-Rheims render clearly different wording with a clearly different meaning. That's a gap in this research pass, not a finding that no such verse exists.

---

## Background

Baruch is a composite of several originally separate works (an opening confession of sin, a Wisdom poem, laments and consolations addressed to Jerusalem, and the Letter of Jeremiah appended as chapter 6), likely composed originally in Hebrew for at least the earlier material, though no Hebrew manuscript of Baruch itself survives — only a small Greek fragment of the Letter of Jeremiah portion has turned up among the Dead Sea Scrolls (7Q2). All the English Catholic translations in common use (RSV2CE, NABRE, Douay-Rheims) present Baruch as a single continuous book with the same basic structure, so I would not expect the kind of large-scale divergence seen in Tobit or Judith here — but I haven't done the verse-by-verse comparison needed to confirm whether smaller wording differences exist.
