# Zechariah: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

Zechariah has two well-known candidate cruxes, described below, but this research pass was not able to confirm exactly how each of the ten tracked translations renders them, so they are presented here as background rather than a fully-sourced split.

---

## Background: two candidate variants worth checking directly

- **Zechariah 11:13** -- MT has the prophet told to throw his wages "to the potter" in the house of the LORD; this is the verse Matthew 27:9-10 draws on for the "potter's field" purchased with Judas's returned silver. The Syriac Peshitta instead reads "treasury" rather than "potter" -- a plausible one-consonant confusion in Hebrew between the words for "potter" (*yotzer*) and "treasury" (*otzar*). This research pass has not confirmed which, if any, of the ten tracked translations reflects the "treasury" reading rather than "potter."
- **Zechariah 12:10** -- MT has the people looking "on me, whom they have pierced" (widely read as messianic and quoted in John 19:37); the Septuagint instead reads something closer to "look on me because they have mocked/danced insultingly," reflecting a different Hebrew verb. This research pass has not confirmed which, if any, of the ten tracked translations reflects the Septuagint's alternative rather than "pierced."
