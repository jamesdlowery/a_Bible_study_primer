# 1 Corinthians: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

A systematic search did not find a case in 1 Corinthians where the ten tracked translations render a verse with different, meaning-changing wording. The well-known variant below is a case where all ten agree on both the wording and its location -- the variation in the wider manuscript tradition is about placement, not wording.

---

## Side note: a passage some manuscripts place elsewhere entirely, though the ten tracked translations agree on both wording and placement

- **1 Corinthians 14:34–35** — these verses (instructing that women "keep silent in the churches") appear after verse 33 in most manuscripts, but after verse 40 in a cluster of Western manuscripts including Codex Bezae. This kind of "floating" text, appearing at two different points across the manuscript tradition, is generally treated by textual critics as a sign the material may have originated as a marginal note. All ten of your translations place the passage after verse 33 with identical wording; several (NIV, ESV, NASB) footnote the alternate placement, while KJV and NKJV do not mention it.
