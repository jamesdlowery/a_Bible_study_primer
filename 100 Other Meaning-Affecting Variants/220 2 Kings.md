# 2 Kings: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

An honest note: 2 Kings 8:26 itself — Ahaziah of Judah's age at accession — is textually stable across witnesses; every translation and every ancient version agrees he was 22. The real split on this same historical detail actually shows up in the parallel account, 2 Chronicles 22:2, where MT gives a different, internally contradictory number ("forty-two"). See the 2 Chronicles document in this collection for that split, verse-quoted in full there.

A systematic search did not find a separate case within 2 Kings itself where the ten tracked translations render a verse with different, meaning-changing wording.
