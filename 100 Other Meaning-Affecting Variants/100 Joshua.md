# Joshua: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

The research pass behind this project did not confirm a case in Joshua where the ten tracked translations render a verse with different wording that changes its meaning. Joshua has several notable structural differences between the Hebrew and Greek textual traditions, but none of them surfaces as a wording split among the ten tracked translations -- they're noted below as background.

---

## Side notes: structural differences in the wider tradition, not reflected as splits among the ten tracked translations

- **Joshua 6** -- the Septuagint's account of the fall of Jericho, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment (4QJoshᵃ), are both somewhat shorter than MT's version. All ten tracked translations follow the longer MT account.
- **Joshua 8:30-35** -- the passage describing the altar built on Mount Ebal appears at three different points in the narrative across MT, the Septuagint, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment. All ten tracked translations follow MT's placement.
- **Joshua 24:33** -- the Septuagint appends an extended ending describing Israel's later lapse into idolatry (worship of Baal and Astarte). None of the ten tracked translations includes this material.
