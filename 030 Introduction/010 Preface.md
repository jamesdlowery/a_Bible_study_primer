# Preface

It is a fact that we have no original Bible manuscripts available to us today, and it is unquestionable that errors have been found in at least some of the manuscript copies that we do have. It also goes without saying that we have different translations, some of which differ in interpretation in at least a few places. How anyone can say with 100% certainty that this particular manuscript copy and/or that particular translation is still the inerrant Word of God, exactly as the Holy Spirit originally inspired ("breathed") the author to record, is a hard pill for me to swallow.

I don't deny God's ability to work such a miracle, but I do deny my own ability to know without a shadow of doubt that any one manuscript and/or translation is it. The best consensus I can come to is that He has allowed manuscripts and translations to exist throughout history so believers have a solid baseline starting point for getting to know Him (see truthischrist.com for some of the reasons I personally choose the KJV -- a personal reading preference, not this book's verdict on which translation is best; see Purpose & Scope). As time has progressed, more manuscripts have been found and more translations have been rendered — some better than others in both cases ("...and knowledge shall be increased." Dan. 12:4).

Beyond trying to find the "best" translation to use as that baseline, it's up to the individual to develop a personal relationship with God and to seek out the 100% Truth. That's why I am open to considering the Bible commentaries of men with differing views, and why I have not been able to formally join any particular church or denomination.

One more thing worth saying plainly: this book's information was gathered with the help of AI, under my direction throughout as Editor (see A Note on Method and Verification for the fuller account).

Don't let manmade doctrine or dogma get in the way of your seeking after the Truth!
