# Preface

It is a fact that we have no original Bible manuscripts available to us today, and it is unquestionable that errors have been found in at least some of the manuscript copies that we do have. It also goes without saying that we have different translations in which some have different interpretations in at least some places. How anyone can say with 100% certainty that this particular manuscript copy and/or that particular translation is still the inerrant Word of God, just as the Holy Spirit originally inspired ('breathed') the author to (have) record(ed), is a hard pill for me to swallow.

I don't deny God's ability to work such a miracle, but I do deny my own ability to know without a shadow of doubt that any one manuscript and/or translation is it. The best consensus I can come to is that He has allowed there to be manuscripts/translations throughout history for believers to have as a solid baseline starting point for getting to know Him (see 'https://truthischrist.com' for one (actually many) of the reasons I choose the KJV, myself); as time has progressed, some more and some better manuscripts have been found and some more and some better translations have been rendered ("...and knowledge shall be increased." Dan. 12:4).

Other than trying to find the 'best' translation to use as that solid baseline, it's up to the individual to have and to develop a personal relationship with God and to seek out the 100% Truth. That's why I am open to considering the Bible commentaries of men with differing views; and, that is why I have not been able to formally join any particular church/denomination.

Don't let manmade doctrine or dogma get in the way of your seeking after the Truth!
