# Song of Songs: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

A systematic search did not find anything in Song of Songs that rises to the tier of significance covered elsewhere in this collection -- a genuine, well-documented manuscript variant (MT vs. Samaritan Pentateuch, Septuagint, or Dead Sea Scrolls) that changes meaning and that shows up as a real difference among the ten tracked translations.

Song of Songs' well-known translation difficulties are almost entirely about rare vocabulary (many words in the book occur nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible, making their precise meaning genuinely uncertain) and about how explicitly or figuratively to render its erotic poetry — real and substantive differences in translation philosophy, but not cases where the underlying manuscripts disagree about the text itself. It would be artificial to manufacture a variant-style entry here to match the format used for other books, so I've left this as a placeholder rather than pad it out.
