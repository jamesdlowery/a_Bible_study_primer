# Leviticus: Textual and Translation Differences

Leviticus is one of the textually calmest books in the Old Testament. No entry is recorded for Leviticus: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. The items below are real variants in the wider manuscript tradition, but none of them produces a split among the tracked translations.

## Side notes: cases where the tracked translations agree, but a real variant exists elsewhere in the manuscript tradition

- **Leviticus 17:3–4** — the Dead Sea Scrolls and Septuagint preserve a longer text specifying additional detail about slaughtering animals outside the camp; MT is shorter at this point. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow the shorter MT reading, and none flags the longer version in a footnote. Of the 17 translations added later, AKJV, ASV, BSB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Smith's Literal Translation, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT were each checked against their own text and all follow the shorter Masoretic wording at 17:3-4, none flagging the longer Greek/Qumran reading in a footnote; CJB, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, RSV, and TRB were not checked for this variant.
- **Leviticus 18:21 / 20:2–5** — these verses concern child sacrifice "to Molech." A small number of Septuagint manuscripts show a scribal-level confusion between "Molech" (a god's name) and a similar-looking Hebrew word meaning something closer to "ruler" or "king," but this is generally regarded as an isolated Greek scribal error rather than a genuine competing Hebrew reading. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all render "Molech"; of the 17 added later, AKJV, ASV, BSB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Smith's Literal Translation, TRB, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT were each checked against their own text and all render "Molech" at both 18:21 and 20:2 (Douay-Rheims and the CPDV in the Vulgate's spelling, "the idol Moloch"); CJB, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, and RSV were not checked for this variant.
