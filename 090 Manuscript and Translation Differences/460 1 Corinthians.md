# 1 Corinthians: Textual and Translation Differences

No entry is recorded for 1 Corinthians: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. The well-known variant below is a case where the tracked translations agree on both the wording and its location -- the variation in the wider manuscript tradition is about placement, not wording.

## Side note: a passage some manuscripts place elsewhere entirely, though the tracked translations agree on both wording and placement

- **1 Corinthians 14:34–35** — these verses (instructing that women "keep silent in the churches") appear after verse 33 in most manuscripts, but after verse 40 in a cluster of Western manuscripts including Codex Bezae. This kind of "floating" text, appearing at two different points across the manuscript tradition, is generally treated by textual critics as a sign the material may have originated as a marginal note. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all place the passage after verse 33 with identical wording; several (NIV, ESV, NASB) footnote the alternate placement, while KJV and NKJV do not mention it. The remaining 17 tracked translations (AKJV, ASV, BSB, CJB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, RSV, Smith's Literal Translation, TRB, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT) were not checked for this variant, though no dissenting placement is expected given how specialized this manuscript question is.
