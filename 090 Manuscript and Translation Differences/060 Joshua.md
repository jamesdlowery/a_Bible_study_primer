# Joshua: Textual and Translation Differences

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE, ASV, WEB, DR, CPDV, Darby, YLT, SLT, WBT, AKJV, NRSV-CE, RSV, NABRE, ERV, NET, BSB, TRB, CJB

The research pass behind this project did not confirm a case in Joshua where the tracked translations render a verse with different wording that changes its meaning. Joshua has several notable structural differences between the Hebrew and Greek textual traditions, but none of them surfaces as a wording split among the tracked translations -- they're noted below as background.

---

## Side notes: structural differences in the wider tradition, not reflected as splits among the tracked translations

- **Joshua 6** -- the Septuagint's account of the fall of Jericho, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment (4QJoshᵃ), are both somewhat shorter than MT's version. The ten originally-tracked translations all follow the longer MT account; this was not independently re-checked across the additional 17 translations in this research pass, though no dissenting English translation is expected given how specialized this structural question is.
- **Joshua 8:30-35** -- the passage describing the altar built on Mount Ebal appears at three different points in the narrative across MT, the Septuagint, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment. The ten originally-tracked translations all follow MT's placement; not independently re-checked across the additional 17.
- **Joshua 24:33** -- the Septuagint appends an extended ending describing Israel's later lapse into idolatry (worship of Baal and Astarte). None of the ten originally-tracked translations includes this material; no English translation checked in this project's wider research has been found to include it either.
