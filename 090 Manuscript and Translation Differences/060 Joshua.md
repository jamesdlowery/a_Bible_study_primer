# Joshua: Textual and Translation Differences

No entry is recorded for Joshua: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. Joshua has several notable structural differences between the Hebrew and Greek textual traditions, but none of them surfaces as a wording split among the tracked translations -- they're noted below as background.

---

## Side notes: structural differences in the wider tradition, not reflected as splits among the tracked translations

- **Joshua 6** -- the Septuagint's account of the fall of Jericho, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment (4QJoshᵃ), are both somewhat shorter than MT's version. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow the longer MT account; of the 17 added later, AKJV, ASV, BSB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Smith's Literal Translation, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT were each checked against their own text and all follow the fuller MT account, each carrying the seven-priests-and-seven-trumpets instruction of 6:4 that the shorter Greek text lacks; CJB, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, and RSV, checked online, likewise carry the seven-priests-and-trumpets instruction of 6:4 (the NABRE with "seven priests carrying ram's horns"); TRB was not checked for this variant.
- **Joshua 8:30-35** -- the passage describing the altar built on Mount Ebal appears at three different points in the narrative across MT, the Septuagint, and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow MT's placement; of the 17 added later, AKJV, ASV, BSB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Smith's Literal Translation, TRB, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT were each checked against their own text and all keep MT's placement, printing 8:30-35 at the end of chapter 8 immediately before 9:1; CJB, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, and RSV, checked online, likewise print 8:30-35 in MT's position at the end of chapter 8.
- **Joshua 24:33** -- the Septuagint appends an extended ending describing Israel's later lapse into idolatry (worship of Baal and Astarte). None of the ten originally-tracked translations includes this material; no English translation checked in this book's wider research has been found to include it either.
