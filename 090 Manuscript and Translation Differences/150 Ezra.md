# Ezra: Textual and Translation Differences

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE, ASV, WEB, DR, CPDV, Darby, YLT, SLT, WBT, AKJV, NRSV-CE, RSV, NABRE, ERV, NET, BSB, TRB, CJB

A systematic search did not find a case in Ezra where the tracked translations render a verse with different, meaning-changing wording. The one well-known puzzle in this book is an internal arithmetic discrepancy in the underlying Hebrew text itself, not a translation difference -- every translation checked renders the numbers exactly as the Hebrew states them.

---

## Side note: an unresolved internal discrepancy, not a translation split

- **Ezra 2:64** — the chapter's itemized, family-by-family list of returning exiles sums to 29,818, but the chapter's own stated grand total is 42,360. The tracked translations all render the numbers exactly as given, without attempting to reconcile the discrepancy (see also the parallel list in Nehemiah 7, which has the same stated total but a different itemized sum). This was not independently re-checked across all 27, though no translation attempting to silently harmonize the numbers is expected, since doing so would require altering the Hebrew figures themselves rather than simply translating them.
