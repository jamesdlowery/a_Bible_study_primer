# 1 Kings: Textual and Translation Differences

No entry is recorded for 1 Kings: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. The two most consequential variants below are cases where the translations agree with each other.

---

## Side notes: real variants in the wider tradition that don't split the tracked translations

- **1 Kings 6:1** -- MT dates the start of Solomon's temple construction to "the 480th year" after the Exodus; the majority of Septuagint manuscripts instead read "the 440th year" (a minority of Greek manuscripts agree with MT). This verse is central to Exodus-dating debates. The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow MT's "480 years"; the remaining 17 tracked translations were not checked for this variant, though no dissenting English translation is expected given how specialized this manuscript question is.
- **1 Kings 12** -- the Septuagint inserts an entire alternate narrative of Jeroboam's rise to power after 12:24, differing substantially in detail from MT's account across chapters 11-12 (different names, different events). This is a structural difference in content, not a wording variant within a shared verse. None of the ten originally-tracked translations includes this alternate material; no English translation checked in this book's wider research has been found to include it either.
