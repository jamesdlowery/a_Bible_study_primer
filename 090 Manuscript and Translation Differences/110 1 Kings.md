# 1 Kings: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

A systematic search did not find a case in 1 Kings where the ten tracked translations render a verse with different, meaning-changing wording. The two most consequential variants below are cases where all ten translations agree with each other.

---

## Side notes: real variants in the wider tradition that don't split the ten tracked translations

- **1 Kings 6:1** -- MT dates the start of Solomon's temple construction to "the 480th year" after the Exodus; the majority of Septuagint manuscripts instead read "the 440th year" (a minority of Greek manuscripts agree with MT). This verse is central to Exodus-dating debates. All ten tracked translations follow MT's "480 years."
- **1 Kings 12** -- the Septuagint inserts an entire alternate narrative of Jeroboam's rise to power after 12:24, differing substantially in detail from MT's account across chapters 11-12 (different names, different events). This is a structural difference in content, not a wording variant within a shared verse. None of the ten tracked translations includes this alternate material.
