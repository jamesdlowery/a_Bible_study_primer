# 2 Kings: Textual and Translation Differences

An honest note: 2 Kings 8:26 itself — Ahaziah of Judah's age at accession — is textually stable across witnesses; every translation and every ancient version agrees he was 22. The real split on this same historical detail actually shows up in the parallel account, 2 Chronicles 22:2, where MT gives a different, internally contradictory number ("forty-two"). See the 2 Chronicles chapter in this book for that split, verse-quoted in full there.

No entry is recorded for 2 Kings: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists.
