---

## Background

1 Maccabees is a historical narrative almost certainly composed originally in Hebrew (a title in Hebrew is reported by Origen), but the Hebrew original is lost, and the book survives complete only in a comparatively stable Greek tradition — without the competing recensions that create real translation splits in Tobit and Judith. I would not expect a large-scale divergence between RSV2CE and NABRE here, since both translate from essentially the same Greek base, but I haven't done the verse-by-verse check needed to confirm no meaningful differences exist, including against Douay-Rheims' and the CPDV's shared Vulgate-based text.
