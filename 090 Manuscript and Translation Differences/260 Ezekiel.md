# Ezekiel: Textual and Translation Differences

No entry is recorded for Ezekiel: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. Ezekiel is one of the books where the Septuagint runs measurably shorter than the Masoretic Text in places (a smaller-scale version of the phenomenon covered for Jeremiah elsewhere in this book), so the underlying manuscript picture is more complex than the absence of an entry might suggest.
