# 1 Chronicles: Significant Textual Variants Across Ten Translations

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE

The two most notable numerical differences involving 1 Chronicles are disagreements between 1 Chronicles and its own parallel passage in 2 Samuel -- not a case where the ten tracked translations render the *same* verse differently from each other. Every one of the ten translations renders each passage exactly as its own underlying Hebrew text states, without attempting to harmonize the two. These are documented here as background, not as splits among the ten tracked translations.

---

## Side notes: real cross-book differences that don't split the ten tracked translations

- **1 Chronicles 21:25 vs. 2 Samuel 24:24** — 1 Chronicles has David pay "six hundred shekels of gold" for the threshing floor site; 2 Samuel 24:24 has him pay "fifty shekels of silver." Many scholars think these describe two different transactions (a smaller immediate purchase of oxen and equipment in Samuel, a larger purchase of the whole hilltop site in Chronicles) rather than a contradiction; a minority view it as a transmission difference. All ten tracked translations render each figure as its own text states, in both books.
- **1 Chronicles 21:5 vs. 2 Samuel 24:9** — the two books give different totals for David's census of fighting men (1,100,000/470,000 in Chronicles vs. 800,000/500,000 in Samuel). All ten tracked translations render each book's own numbers without harmonizing them.
