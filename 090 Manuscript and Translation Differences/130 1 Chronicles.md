# 1 Chronicles: Textual and Translation Differences

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE, ASV, WEB, DR, CPDV, Darby, YLT, SLT, WBT, AKJV, NRSV-CE, RSV, NABRE, ERV, NET, BSB, TRB, CJB

The two most notable numerical differences involving 1 Chronicles are disagreements between 1 Chronicles and its own parallel passage in 2 Samuel -- not a case where the tracked translations render the *same* verse differently from each other. Every translation renders each passage exactly as its own underlying Hebrew text states, without attempting to harmonize the two. These are documented here as background, not as splits among the tracked translations.

---

## Side notes: real cross-book differences that don't split the tracked translations

- **1 Chronicles 21:25 vs. 2 Samuel 24:24** — 1 Chronicles has David pay "six hundred shekels of gold" for the threshing floor site; 2 Samuel 24:24 has him pay "fifty shekels of silver." Many scholars think these describe two different transactions (a smaller immediate purchase of oxen and equipment in Samuel, a larger purchase of the whole hilltop site in Chronicles) rather than a contradiction; a minority view it as a transmission difference. The tracked translations all render each figure as its own text states, in both books; this pattern was not independently re-checked across all 27, though no translation harmonizing the two figures is expected or has been found.
- **1 Chronicles 21:5 vs. 2 Samuel 24:9** — the two books give different totals for David's census of fighting men (1,100,000/470,000 in Chronicles vs. 800,000/500,000 in Samuel). The tracked translations all render each book's own numbers without harmonizing them; not independently re-checked across all 27.
