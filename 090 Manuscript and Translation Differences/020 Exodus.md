# Exodus: Textual and Translation Differences

**Translations covered:** AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, RSV2CE, ASV, WEB, DR, CPDV, Darby, YLT, SLT, WBT, AKJV, NRSV-CE, RSV, NABRE, ERV, NET, BSB, TRB, CJB

No entry is recorded for Exodus: no verse was identified where the 27 tracked translations render the same words with a different, meaning-changing sense. The absence of an entry marks the limit of the search, not proof that no such variant exists. ## Side note: a case where the tracked translations agree, but a real variant exists elsewhere in the manuscript tradition

- **Exodus 1:5** — MT gives the number of Jacob's household who went down to Egypt as "seventy"; the Septuagint and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment instead give "seventy-five" -- the figure Stephen uses when he quotes this same tradition in Acts 7:14. (Deuteronomy 10:22's parallel statement of the same fact is textually stable at "seventy" in both MT and the Septuagint, so this specific variant is confined to Exodus 1:5 itself, not the wider tradition about Jacob's household.) All ten originally-tracked translations follow MT's "seventy" at Exodus 1:5, and all ten likewise render Acts 7:14's "seventy-five" as the New Testament text itself states it, without harmonizing the two numbers against each other. This is not independently re-confirmed across the additional 17 translations in this research pass, though no dissenting reading was identified.
- **Exodus 12:40** — MT states the Israelites lived "in Egypt" 430 years; the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch both add "and in Canaan," splitting the 430 years between the two locations (relevant to a longstanding chronological question about how long the Egyptian sojourn actually lasted, compared with Genesis' genealogical data). All ten originally-tracked translations follow MT's "in Egypt" alone, without the addition. This was directly re-confirmed for ASV, WEB, Darby, Douay-Rheims, the BSB, the ERV, the AKJV, and the CJB, all of which likewise read "in Egypt" alone -- no dissenting translation was found among the 27 checked.
