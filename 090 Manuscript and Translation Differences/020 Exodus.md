## Side note: a case where the tracked translations agree, but a real variant exists elsewhere in the manuscript tradition

- **Exodus 1:5** — MT gives the number of Jacob's household who went down to Egypt as "seventy"; the Septuagint and a Dead Sea Scrolls fragment instead give "seventy-five" -- the figure Stephen uses when he quotes this same tradition in Acts 7:14. (Deuteronomy 10:22's parallel statement of the same fact is textually stable at "seventy" in both MT and the Septuagint, so this specific variant is confined to Exodus 1:5 itself, not the wider tradition about Jacob's household.) The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow MT's "seventy" at Exodus 1:5, and all ten likewise render Acts 7:14's "seventy-five" as the New Testament text itself states it, without harmonizing the two numbers against each other. Of the 17 translations added later, AKJV, ASV, BSB, CPDV, Darby, Douay-Rheims, Smith's Literal Translation, TRB, WEB, Webster's Bible, and YLT were each checked against their own text and all read "seventy" at Exodus 1:5, BSB with a footnote recording the Dead Sea Scrolls and Septuagint "seventy-five"; CJB, ERV, NABRE, NET Bible, NRSV-CE, and RSV, checked online, likewise all read "seventy," the NABRE footnoting the Septuagint's seventy-five.
- **Exodus 12:40** — MT states the Israelites lived "in Egypt" 430 years; the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch both add "and in Canaan," splitting the 430 years between the two locations (relevant to a longstanding chronological question about how long the Egyptian sojourn actually lasted, compared with Genesis' genealogical data). The ten translations tracked first (AMP, CSB, ESV, KJV, NKJV, NASB, LSB, NIV, NLT, and RSV2CE) all follow MT's "in Egypt" alone, without the addition. So do ASV, WEB, Darby, Douay-Rheims, the BSB, the ERV, the AKJV, and the CJB, all of which likewise read "in Egypt" alone -- no dissenting translation was found among the 27 checked.
