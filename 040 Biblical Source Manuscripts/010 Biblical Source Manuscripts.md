# Biblical Source Manuscripts

## Hebrew Bible / Old Testament (Masoretic tradition and related)

- **Biblia Hebraica Stuttgartensia (BHS)** – the long-standard diplomatic edition based on the Leningrad Codex, with a full critical apparatus of variants from other Masoretic manuscripts, the Septuagint, Dead Sea Scrolls (as then known), Samaritan Pentateuch, Targums, Peshitta, and Vulgate.
- **Biblia Hebraica Quinta (BHQ)** – the ongoing successor to BHS (fascicles published book-by-book). More extensive apparatus, fuller treatment of the Dead Sea Scrolls, and updated evaluations.
- **Hebrew University Bible Project (HUBP)** – diplomatic edition of the Aleppo Codex with a very detailed apparatus.

## Septuagint (LXX)

- **Rahlfs-Hanhart Septuaginta** (2 vols., 2006 revision) – the standard hand edition for most purposes.
- **Göttingen Septuagint** (Septuaginta: Vetus Testamentum Graecum) – the definitive multi-volume critical edition with exhaustive apparatus (still incomplete for a few books).
- **Cambridge Larger Septuagint** (Brooke-McLean) – older but still useful for some books.

## Dead Sea Scrolls (biblical manuscripts)

- **Discoveries in the Judaean Desert (DJD)** series – the official editio princeps of virtually all the scrolls.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls Bible** (Abegg, Flint, Ulrich) and the more technical **Biblical Qumran Scrolls** volumes – convenient collections of the biblical texts with notes.
- Online: the Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library and the Brill *Dead Sea Scrolls Electronic Library*.

## Samaritan Pentateuch

- A. von Gall, *Der hebräische Pentateuch der Samaritaner* (1914–1918) – classic critical edition.
- More recent: A. Tal & M. Florentin, *The Samaritan Pentateuch* (2010) and the ongoing critical work associated with the *Biblia Hebraica Quinta* apparatus and specialized monographs.

## Aramaic Targums

- Critical editions vary by Targum:

 - Onqelos & Jonathan: Sperber’s *The Bible in Aramaic*.
 - Palestinian Targums (Neofiti, Fragment Targums, etc.): the *Editio princeps* volumes and the *Aramaic Bible* series.
 - Pseudo-Jonathan: specialized critical editions.

- Comprehensive modern collections appear in the *Aramaic Bible* project (Michael Glazier / Liturgical Press).

## Syriac Peshitta (Old Testament)

**Leiden Peshitta** (*The Old Testament in Syriac according to the Peshitta Version*) – the standard critical edition (Institute of Leiden), published book-by-book with apparatus.

## Latin Vulgate

- **Biblia Sacra iuxta Vulgatam Versionem** (Weber-Gryson / Stuttgart Vulgate, 5th ed.) – the standard critical edition of Jerome’s Vulgate with apparatus of early manuscripts.
- For the older Latin (Vetus Latina): the *Vetus Latina* series (Beuron) and the *Vetus Latina Database*.

## New Testament

- **Nestle-Aland Novum Testamentum Graece, 28th edition (NA28)** – the primary modern critical/eclectic text with the fullest apparatus of Greek manuscripts, versions, and Fathers.

- **United Bible Societies Greek New Testament, 5th edition (UBS5)** – essentially the same text as NA28 but with an apparatus oriented toward translators (fewer variants, graded by certainty).

- **Byzantine / Majority Text**:

 - Robinson-Pierpont, *The New Testament in the Original Greek: Byzantine Textform* (2005/2018).
 - Hodges-Farstad, *The Greek New Testament According to the Majority Text*.

- **Textus Receptus**:

Scrivener’s edition (the form underlying the KJV) and the various Elzevir / Stephanus editions; modern reprints with apparatus notes are available.

- **International Greek New Testament Project (IGNTP)** and the ongoing *Editio Critica Maior* (ECM) – the most exhaustive apparatus for the books already published (Acts, Catholic Epistles, etc.).

## Useful digital hubs

- **Academic Bible / Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft** platforms (BHS, BHQ, NA28, etc.).
- **Accordance, Logos, BibleWorks** (legacy), and **STEP Bible** – all integrate many of the above editions with searchable apparatuses.
- **Center for the Study of New Testament Manuscripts (CSNTM)** and the **Institut für Neutestamentliche Textforschung (INTF)** – manuscript images and the *Kurzgefasste Liste*.
- **Göttingen Septuagint** and **Leiden Peshitta** have growing digital presence through their publishers.
